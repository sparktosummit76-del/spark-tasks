# Problem brief – Spark Tasks (Day 1)

**Target user:** a part-time student who also works (persona "Asha", see PRD).
**Pain point:** tasks are scattered across WhatsApp notes, paper and memory; deadlines are missed.
**Current workaround:** a notes app with an unsorted list; no priorities, no due dates, no weekly view.
**Desired outcome:** one place to capture tasks in under 10 seconds and see what matters this week.
**Measurable benefit:** fewer missed deadlines (target: zero overdue high-priority tasks per week) and
about 15 minutes saved each week on planning (measured on Day 5 with the Weekly Digest run log).
**Project boundary (in scope):** add, edit, complete, delete, prioritise and date personal tasks in one browser.
**Out of scope for Milestone 1:** accounts, sharing, sync across devices, notifications, AI features.
